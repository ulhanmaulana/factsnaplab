Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Factsnap Lab produces short educational videos. "Factsnap Lab Uploader" is an internal tool used only by the Factsnap Lab team to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

What data the uploader uses

  • TikTok: with our own authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, username) of the Factsnap Lab TikTok account, and uses the video.upload / video.publish permissions only to send our own videos to that account.
  • YouTube, Instagram, Facebook: access tokens for our own channel and pages, used only to upload our own videos and read their basic statistics.

The tool does not collect, process or store data about viewers or any other TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook users.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the Factsnap Lab production computer, are never shared, sold or published, and can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

Third parties

Videos are sent only to the official APIs of TikTok, Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram, Facebook). We do not use advertising or tracking services.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms and does not collect personal data.

Your choices

If you believe our content involves your personal data, contact us and we will review and remove it where appropriate.

Contact

Contact us through a direct message on any of our official accounts listed on the home page.
